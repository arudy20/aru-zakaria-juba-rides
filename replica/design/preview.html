Juba Rides design system

Built for a phone held in bright sun on a bumpy road: white backgrounds, deep ink text, one highly legible typeface, and buttons big enough to hit in a moving car. The colours come from the Nile and Juba's red laterite roads. Working values until the brand step.

Type

Rules every screen follows

RuleWhy
Nothing tappable under 48 px; the main action is 56 px, full width, at the bottomRiders tap in moving cars on rough roads, often one-handed
White backgrounds and deep ink text; muted grey only for secondary factsReadable in direct sun on low-cost screens
Every server action shows loading and offline states, and keeps its labelNetworks in Juba drop often; people must know what happened
Pickup is a map pin plus a landmark noteMany places have no usable street address
Colour never carries meaning alone; always a word or shape tooColour-blind riders and glare
Icons from Lucide (open licence); no copied icons, illustrations or wordingThe app's look and words are its own
