Juba Rides customer app

A clickable preview of the booking flow in the phone app. Tap through it the way a rider would.

  1. Tap the map (or drag it) to put the red pin on the pickup spot.
  2. Do the same for the destination.
  3. See the fixed fares and book.
  4. Watch a pretend driver accept, arrive and finish the trip.

This is a simulation: the map is a drawing of Juba, the driver is pretend, and prices use the same placeholder rates as the database (car 3,000 SSP + 1,500 per km, rounded up to the nearest 100).
